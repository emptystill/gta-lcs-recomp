New: Liberty City Stories on PC

- The first release of the native PC port of Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories, built from your own copy of the PS2 game. You need the USA disc (SLUS-21423) as an `.iso` file. Nothing of the game is in this download: the launcher builds the game from your disc on your PC.
- Windows, Linux (AppImage) and macOS on Apple Silicon (.dmg).
- The story plays from the start, with the PS2 version's look by default. Each of the improvements below is an option.

The picture

- Widescreen without stretching: a 16:9 (or wider) screen shows more to the sides. In the game's pause menu → Display: **Aspect ratio** (4:3 by default, as on the PS2) and **Widescreen HUD**, which keeps the radar and the HUD in shape and at the screen's edges.
- High frame rates: in the pause menu → Display, **Frame rate cap** (60 fps by default; up to your display's refresh, or no cap). Particles, smoke and fire keep their PS2 speed and size at any frame rate.
- Loading screens and menus can fill a widescreen picture without cutting it off: the launcher's Game page → **Wide menus and loading screens** (off by default).
- Optional HD HUD textures from a high-resolution pack (**HD HUD textures**, off by default) and a sharper HUD (**Sharp HUD**, off by default). The PS2's picture-wide blur can be turned off (**PS2 blur filter**, on by default).
- The intro movies play as on the PS2. **Skip intro movies** in the launcher's Game page goes straight to the game.

Controls

- Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, the mouse turns the camera on foot and in vehicles, left click fires, F gets into vehicles. Every key can be rebound in the launcher's Game page.
- Free aim with the mouse (**Free aim**, on by default): aim the gun where the camera looks, as on PC. The sniper rifle's and the rocket launcher's scopes turn with the mouse, and the wheel zooms them.
- The mouse wheel changes the radio station in vehicles.
- A gamepad works as a PS2 pad. Button prompts in help text and the menus show the keys or buttons of the device you use.
- The pause menu works with the mouse and keyboard: Enter and Escape, tabs and options by click, the wheel, the map's zoom.

The trainer

- F5 opens a trainer: any weapon, the cheats' weapon sets, unlimited ammo, no reload, god mode, wanted level, money, spawning vehicles, repairs, teleports to landmarks, time of day, weather and game speed.

Settings

- The four you change most are in the game's own pause menu: Aspect ratio, Widescreen HUD and Frame rate cap under Display, Master volume under Audio.
- Everything else is in the launcher's **Game** page, in English or Spanish. The old in-game settings menu (F2) is off by default and can be turned on there.

Known problems

- Mission timers and bars, and some effects (lightning, clouds, camera shake), may run faster above 30 fps. If a mission's timer runs out too fast, set **Frame rate cap** to 30 for that mission.
- In very big fights with many explosions, the frame rate can still drop to about 60 fps.
- The game froze once in a long night-time gunfight. If it happens to you, wait about 10 seconds before closing the game: it writes a report to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\LCSRecomp\logs` (Windows). Please attach that file, or the zip from the launcher's report button, to a bug report.
- The sound still runs through the PS2's emulated sound processor and hasn't been checked by ear everywhere.

Launcher

- The launcher finds your disc, downloads the build tools it needs (about 190 MB the first time on Windows), builds the game from your disc and starts it. It updates itself and the game, and backs up your saves before every change. It is in English or Spanish.

Credits

- Built on the [GTA: Vice City Stories native port](https://github.com/gothamor/gta-vcs-recomp) by gothamor, and on [PS2Recomp](https://github.com/ran-j/PS2Recomp) by ran-j and its contributors.

Installing: on Windows, extract the zip into a folder of its own and start `LCSLauncher.exe`. It asks for your disc image and builds the game; the first build takes a while (tens of minutes on most PCs). Your saves are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\LCSRecomp`. On Linux, download the AppImage, make it executable and start it; on macOS, open the .dmg and drag the launcher into Applications.
